《找不同》隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 22 日 / Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. 遊戲資料

《找不同 Spot Difference》會在你的裝置本機儲存已完成的關卡及遊戲進度，用於顯示與恢復遊戲狀態。核心遊戲不需要帳號或連線到開發者伺服器。現有版本未將這些本機進度傳送到開發者伺服器。

2. 廣告與第三方分析

目前送審的 iOS 版本未整合第三方廣告 SDK 或第三方分析 SDK。若未來新增相關服務，我們將在推出前更新本政策及必要的平台隱私權揭露。

3. 主動聯絡客服

如你自行以電子郵件聯繫客服，我們會收到你提供的電子郵件地址及信件內容，僅用來回覆與處理問題，不會出售這些資料。請避免提供密碼、付款資訊及不必要的敏感資料。

4. 資料保存與刪除

遊戲進度存在裝置的 App 資料內，刪除 App 或其資料可能清除進度且無法復原。如需查詢或要求刪除你先前寄給客服的信件資料，請寄信至 kevin0926326@gmail.com。

5. 系統服務與聯絡方式

Apple 可能依其自身隱私政策處理 App Store 下載及系統診斷資訊。開發者聯絡信箱：kevin0926326@gmail.com。

Spot Difference Privacy Policy

Local game data

Spot Difference stores completed levels and game progress locally on your device to provide and resume gameplay. Core gameplay does not require an account or a developer-operated server. The current release does not transmit this on-device progress to the developer.

Advertising and analytics

The current iOS release does not integrate a third-party advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK. If data practices change in a future version, we will update this policy and the applicable store disclosures before release.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive the email address and content you provide voluntarily and use that information to respond to your request. We do not sell it. Please do not provide passwords, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Storage and deletion

Local progress is stored in the app data on your device. Deleting the app or its data may remove progress permanently. To request information about or deletion of a prior support email, contact kevin0926326@gmail.com.

Platform services

Apple may process App Store download and system diagnostic information under Apple's own privacy policy. Contact: kevin0926326@gmail.com.
