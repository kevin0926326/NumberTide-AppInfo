English — Support

Spot Difference is a visual puzzle game with 30 illustrated scenes. Compare the two pictures side by side and tap the differences to complete each level. There is no countdown timer.

For bug reports, gameplay questions, or feedback, contact kevin0926326@gmail.com. Please include the level number, app version, device and iOS version, and reproduction steps when possible. Do not send passwords or sensitive information.

Level progress is stored on the device and may be lost when app data is deleted. Read our Privacy Policy.
