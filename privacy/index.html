隱私政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 20 日

本政策說明 iOS 遊戲《數字潮汐》（Number Tide）如何處理遊戲資料。開發者聯絡信箱：kevin0926326@gmail.com。

1. 遊戲內的資料

遊戲會在你的裝置本機保存最高分、最高合成數字、最多合併次數、已完成局數、音效與語言偏好。這些資料用於顯示個人紀錄、恢復偏好與改善遊戲體驗。遊戲基本功能不需要帳號或網路連線。依目前 v1.0 的程式設計，開發者沒有專用伺服器接收這些本機紀錄。

2. 第三方服務與分享

本遊戲目前不整合廣告平台或第三方分析 SDK。若日後啟用 Apple Game Center 排行榜，並在使用者登入或使用相關功能時，排名及分數等資訊可能由 Apple 依其服務政策處理。本遊戲目前尚未對外啟用排行榜入口。當使用者主動點選「分享成績」時，iOS 分享選單會讓使用者自行選擇分享的目的地；開發者不會因為產生成績圖片而自動收取圖片。

3. 客服聯絡

若你主動寄電子郵件給客服，我們會取得你在信件中提供的電子郵件地址及內容，僅用於回覆與處理問題，不會出售這些資料。請勿在客服信件內提供密碼、付款資訊或不必要的敏感資訊。

4. 保留、刪除與選擇

本機紀錄保留於使用者裝置的 App 資料中，通常可透過刪除 App（及相關 App 資料）清除；刪除 App 可能使紀錄無法復原。對於寄送的客服郵件，可寄信至 kevin0926326@gmail.com 申請查詢或刪除，並請提供足以識別信件的資訊。Apple 或使用者選擇的分享服務有各自的資料管理與刪除規則。

5. 變更與聯絡方式

若未來加入廣告、分析、線上同步或其他資料處理功能，我們會在適用時更新政策及 App Store 隱私申報。聯絡信箱：kevin0926326@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

On-device data

Number Tide stores your high score, highest number, most merges, completed game count, sound setting, and language preference locally on your device. These records support personal progress and settings. Core gameplay works offline without an account. The current v1.0 design has no developer-operated server collecting these local records.

Third-party services and sharing

The current app has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. If Apple Game Center leaderboards are enabled in a future release, Apple may process submitted scores and leaderboard details under its policies when players use that service. Leaderboard controls are not enabled in the current first-release candidate. A user may choose to share a score image through the iOS share sheet; creating that image does not automatically send it to the developer.

Support email

If you email support, we receive the email address and content you voluntarily provide and use them to answer your request. We do not sell this information. Do not send passwords, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain in the app's on-device data and can usually be deleted by removing the app and its data; deleted progress may not be recoverable. For inquiries about an email you sent, contact kevin0926326@gmail.com to request access or deletion. Apple and any sharing destination govern their own data handling.

Updates

If features or data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated as appropriate. Contact: kevin0926326@gmail.com.
